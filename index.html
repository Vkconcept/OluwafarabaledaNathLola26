Our Story

How We Met

Damilola & Nathaniel

It all started

in the choir.

✦

We met in the choir, where we started out simply serving together and sharing our love for music.

What began as a friendship gradually grew into something deeper and more beautiful than we could have imagined.

For ten years, we have shared different seasons together the laughter, the memories, the growth, and all the little moments that have brought us closer.

Looking back, we are grateful for how far we have come. What started in the choir has brought us to this beautiful moment, and we are excited to begin our next chapter together as husband and wife.
